Worth Knowing Before You Go

Tartine has been a Mission District fixture for years, and it shows in the line that often forms before opening. The bakery leans into what it does best — naturally leavened breads and laminated pastries — rather than trying to be everything to everyone.

If you're coming for bread, later mornings tend to have the fullest selection, though popular items can sell out by early afternoon on weekends. There's a mix of indoor counter seating and a few outdoor spots if the weather's nice, but it's a popular corner, so don't expect to camp out with a laptop — there's no wifi, and it's better suited to a quick stop than a long work session.
